Press record and watch the words land.

Aloe Scribe captures your microphone and the call on separate channels. It works with Zoom, Teams, Meet or any app that plays sound. The live transcript shows it is working, and the Mic and Call bars move only for sound that is being heard and transcribed.

3Weeks later

Ask, and see the quotes before the answer.

Ask notes searches every meeting by words and by meaning, along with the contact pages and documents in your notes folder. The quotes appear the moment you press Ask. Then a model on your computer writes the answer under them, with numbered sources.

Follow-ups keep the thread. Ask it to draft a two-line email to Sam and it knows which Sam. No question and no note leaves the computer, and a question costs nothing.

Your notes are files

Every meeting is a file you already know how to open.

Each note is plain Markdown in a folder you choose, including a OneDrive or SharePoint folder. Here is the same supplier review four ways, in real screenshots.

1As a file

Plain Markdown with a header you can search.

Every note starts with the same header of title, date, client, attendees and speakers, with names spelled the same way every time. A question like the last time you met the co-op is answered from the headers alone, without reading a transcript.

Nothing about the file is locked to Aloe Scribe. Obsidian and Logseq open the folder as it is. Notion, Apple Notes and OneNote bring the files in through their Markdown import.

3In Apple Notes

Or read it like any other note.

Apple Notes imports Markdown on macOS 26 and iOS 26. On a Mac, choose File, Import to Notes and pick the files. Headings and lists come through as rich text, and action items become checkboxes you can tick. The header lines arrive as plain text above the title.

Your notes folder stays the original. Re-import a note when it changes.

Built for your AI

Hand your meetings to the AI you already use.

Cloud notetakers keep your meetings on their servers, and your assistant reaches them through the vendor's connector, with plan limits and a login. Aloe Scribe gives your AI apps a direct line to the notes on your computer, and lets you choose which model does the writing.

Connect Claude, Copilot or Cursor

Aloe Scribe is also an MCP server. In Settings, AI apps, one click connects Claude Code, and Copy setup covers Claude Desktop, Copilot in VS Code and Cursor. The app gets five read-only tools.

  • search_notesKeyword and meaning search, with cited excerpts
  • list_meetingsMeetings by person, topic and date
  • list_action_itemsAction items for a date range
  • get_noteA note's header and summary
  • get_transcriptWhat was said, from a timestamp on

Aloe runs the search on your computer and hands over only the passages that answer, so the app spends a fraction of the tokens it would on whole files.

If the AI app uses a cloud model, that model sees the passages it asked for. Your audio and the rest of your notes stay put.

Settings

Bring your own model

The built-in model writes summaries and Ask notes answers on your computer, sized to its memory. You can point both at your own server instead, such as Ollama or LM Studio on the same machine, or any server that speaks the OpenAI chat format, including a company AI platform.

Customize adds your own standing rules on top of the summary instructions, so every note comes out the way your team writes.

A localhost address keeps everything on the computer. Any other address receives the transcripts it summarizes, so pick a server your company has approved.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is this legal to use?

Aloe Scribe is a recording tool, and recording laws depend on where you and the other participants are. Some places require everyone's consent, others require one party's. Running locally does not change those rules, so disclose that you are recording where the law or your professional obligations require it. When in doubt, ask, the same as you would with any recorder. We keep a plain-language map of recording laws with links to the authoritative sources.

Why is there no bot?

A bot exists so a vendor's server can hear your meeting. Aloe Scribe transcribes on your computer, so it does not need one. It captures the audio your computer is already playing and the audio your mic is already hearing. Nothing joins the call, so there is no extra name in the participant list, and it works in meetings you don't host. You still tell people you are recording, as you would with any notetaker.

Why does local matter?

Cloud notetaker vendors are now facing wiretap class actions because their servers received meeting audio. Universities blocklist notetaker bots, and Teams can flag or deny them. In one survey, 84 percent of professionals said they self-censor when an AI notetaker joins the call. With Aloe Scribe there is no upload, so it is structurally impossible for us to see your data, and nothing is ever used for training.

Does it work with Teams, Zoom, and Meet?

Yes. Aloe Scribe captures system audio, so it works with any app that plays sound. That includes Teams, Zoom, Meet, Webex, Slack huddles, phone calls routed through your computer, and webinars. It also records in-person meetings with the mic alone.

What does it cost?

It is currently free while in beta. Pricing will be announced. There is no account, so there is nothing to sign up for now and nothing that expires.

How does it compare to Granola, Otter or MacWhisper?

Granola captures locally like we do, then sends your audio to cloud services for transcription and enhancement. Otter is a cloud notetaker whose bot joins the call. MacWhisper is a strong local app for files and dictation. Aloe Scribe keeps everything on the machine and is built for meetings. The full comparisons are here, vs Granola, vs Otter, and vs MacWhisper.

Does it work offline?

Only to download the app and its models. After installation, recording, transcription, speaker identification, summaries and Ask notes all work offline.

What format are the transcripts?

Plain Markdown files with a structured metadata header, saved to a folder you choose. Obsidian and Logseq open them as they are, Notion, Apple Notes and OneNote import them, and they are built to feed your own AI tools, including Glean and Copilot. Linked names for Obsidian and Logseq are one switch in Settings.

Can Claude, Copilot, or ChatGPT read my notes?

Yes. Your notes are ordinary files in your own folder, so any AI tool that can read a folder can read them. Microsoft Copilot can index them from OneDrive or SharePoint. For Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Copilot in VS Code and Cursor, the built-in MCP server in Settings, AI apps lets them search the notes and read only the passages they need. You don't need an API key or a vendor account, and you don't hit a plan limit. To keep the questions on your computer too, use Ask notes in the app.

Can I use my own model?

Yes. Summaries and Ask notes can use any server that speaks the OpenAI chat format, such as Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM or a company AI platform. Set it in Settings, Summaries, Model. A localhost address keeps everything on the computer. Any other address receives the transcripts it summarizes, so use one your company has approved.
